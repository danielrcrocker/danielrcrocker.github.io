Teaching & Mentoring

Teaching, mentorship & science communication

I value opportunities to make science accessible, support developing scientists, and connect research with students and broader communities.

Teaching

Connecting chemistry across scales

My goal as an educator is to design student-centered learning experiences that teach students to apply fundamental scientific principles to address real-world problems ranging from air pollution and climate change to planetary evolution. My educational approach is grounded in evidence-based teaching practices developed through formal training in college-level pedagogy and refined through my undergraduate teaching experiences serving as Instructor of Record for General Chemistry and teaching 25 sections across 5 different chemistry courses.

Courses of interest: General Chemistry, Analytical Chemistry, Environmental Chemistry, Atmospheric Chemistry, Isotope Geochemistry, and Earth/Planetary Science.

Mentoring

Supporting developing scientists

I view mentorship as a progression toward scientific independence. Early in a project, I work closely with students to establish goals, experimental skills, and expectations; as their expertise develops, I progressively transfer responsibility for experimental design, data interpretation, and scientific communication. I have directly mentored ten undergraduate researchers, most of whom subsequently pursued graduate study.

GEMS — Geosciences Education & Mentorship Support

I participate annually as a mentor in the GEMS program, supporting students as they navigate graduate education, applications, fellowships, and professional development.

Science Communication & Outreach

Harvard Science Education Partners

Through the Harvard Museums of Science & Culture Science Education Partners program, I participate in virtual classroom visits that connect grade-school students directly with working scientists. These conversations give me an opportunity to introduce students to my research and discuss how scientific questions are developed and investigated.
